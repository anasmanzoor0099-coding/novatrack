Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to NovaTrack. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password (encrypted).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our platform.
  • Order Data: Tracking information you submit.
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device type.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Improve user experience
  • Send important notifications about your account
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including bcrypt password hashing and encrypted connections, to protect your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only when required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or through our platform.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@novatrack.com
